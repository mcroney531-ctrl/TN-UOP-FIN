WN7 recovery: what good looks like, and what to do when it breaks down

1The decision sequence: what to look for on the call

RecognizeRecognitionSpots the WN7 pattern; moves to verify
VerifyVerificationChecks eligibility; reads the result correctly
DecideDecisionPicks the path that fits the verified result
CompleteRecoveryBoth applicable transactions, or owned, documented follow-up
Explain & DocumentCommunicationDone, pending, next stepDocumentationActions, open work, owner

A recorded serial check isn't proof. It shows a check happened, not that the Guide saw or understood the result. Listen for what they did next.

EligibleApproved recovery: replacement and applicable Plus action, or documented follow-up for what's unresolved.
Not eligibleStandard support. No replacement promised on product name alone.
Unclear or unavailable resultDon't guess. Use the established support or escalation route.
