## Ownership

### Ownership & Decision Authority
Who owns the work, who provides evidence, who validates procedures, and who authorizes progression
Proposed responsibilities requiring organizational confirmation, not existing commitments.
### Rollout Decision Authority
Care leadership owns the final proceed / adjust / pause decision at each gate, informed by:
[TABLE]
Partner | Contribution
Quality | Evaluates Guide execution against the six indicators, compares baseline and pilot findings, identifies recurring gaps, and recommends whether performance supports expansion.
Workforce | Evaluates staffing coverage, queues, wait times, abandonment, and scheduled practice capacity; approves each wave's composition, timing, practice coverage, and operational feasibility.
Learning | Confirms instructional readiness, examines practice and coaching feedback, identifies design changes, and recommends refinements.
Specialist Care | Validates recovery procedures, identifies complex recovery issues, and evaluates whether Specialist support remains sustainable.
Care leadership | Weighs the evidence, resolves competing priorities, and authorizes proceeding, adjusting, or pausing.
[/TABLE]
Expansion requires both: Care leadership decides that evidence supports expansion, and Workforce approves the wave's composition, timing, scheduled practice coverage, and operational feasibility.

### Specialist Capacity Approvals
Mateo (Specialist Care) approves Specialist involvement separately for:
- (a) the Lead working meeting, and
- (b) temporary shared-thread support.
Neither approval implies the other.
### Shared Lead Thread Administration
Care leadership designates a participating Lead as the temporary thread Admin; the specific person is confirmed during Prepare. The Admin:
- Coordinates questions and routes blockers to the appropriate owners.
- Tracks unresolved issues and recurring patterns.
- Circulates verified answers to participating Leads.
- Doesn't personally own every procedural resolution.
The thread is for Lead coordination. It doesn't replace live Guide → Lead support or established escalation routes, and it isn't a permanent new support channel. Specialist participation stays conditional on capacity, with no guaranteed real-time response.
### Sustainment Ownership
Instructional maintenance is separate from operational accuracy
- Learning: maintains the Guide Call Path, digital scenario, huddle slide, Lead one-pager, Coaching Cues, and associated learning materials.
- Quality: monitors recurring execution gaps through existing reviews and shares findings for coaching or resource updates; validates relevant operational content in the Lead resources, with Specialist Care.
- Care Systems: validates Orbit access, tool behavior, and relevant system instructions.
- Specialist Care / appropriate policy owners: validate recovery procedures and Plus handling within their authority.
- Care leadership and Leads: reinforce approved guidance through team communication and existing coaching.
- Beacon: remains the authoritative location for approved procedures.
Learning doesn't independently approve operational policy.
