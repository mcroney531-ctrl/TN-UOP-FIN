Lead alignment

What leads know

  • (Experienced leads-- not all leads) Awareness of the problem
  • Existence of available resources/tools (not necessarily the "when" and "how" behind them, along with smaller distinctions, and reliably knowing their locations)

What leads need

  • "When I coach, I need a before and after. What did the customer say, what did the Guide do, and what should they try next time? I can do that in five minutes if I have a clip and a cue."
  • "But I could not tell them which Beacon article to use. I wrote the temporary specialist page name on a whiteboard, and it is not visible to everyone."
  • Cues-- "Leads need a tiny cue for what to listen for."
  • Common requests were: a one-page coaching cue, one strong and one weak call excerpt, a clear answer for pending Plus status, and a way to tell whether the Guide had actually used the serial screen.
  • "Seven respondents said they had shared the Beacon article. Four said they had run a huddle or planned one. Three said they had used a call example. Several said this was harder to track than an assigned Sana module because shifts and local coverage differ."

Team leads working meeting

Meeting agenda

  • Situation brief and WN7 refresh (3 mins)
  • Case Review & Coaching Alignment (19 mins)
  • Discuss the cases (9 mins) — What happened and why?
  • Case A: Strong recovery with a Plus complication
  • Case B: Serial checked, but generic troubleshooting continued
  • Align on expectations (10 mins) — What should Guides do, and how should Leads coach or escalate?
  • Takeaway resource, expectations, next steps (3 mins)

Meeting Objectives

  • Establish understanding
  • Refresher on the basics
  • What's the situation? What has been noted?
  • Current state of things/needs
  • Biggest hangups/hiccups observed so far
  • Hiccups with Plus get a mention here
  • Mention the beacon article, search troubles
  • Establish alignment
  • Coaching outcome: Leads can recognize where Guide execution broke down, investigate whether the cause is a knowledge gap, Orbit interpretation issue, or access/process blocker, and determine the appropriate coaching or escalation response.
  • Specialist-supported small-group case discussion, followed by shared alignment on Guide expectations and coaching responses.*
    *Part of the continuous 19-minute case discussion and alignment, not an extra activity. Specialist availability is a staffing dependency (needs Specialist Care sign-off), not a confirmed commitment.
  • Agree on the Guide recovery sequence, what Leads should reinforce in huddles, and how to handle or escalate unresolved Plus questions.
  • Confirm where to find BH-4419 and the Orbit serial tool; clarify when Guides should use each.
  • Agree on coaching cues
    For each cue, discuss what Leads should listen for, what they should check, and whether the appropriate response is coaching or escalation.

Takeaway resources for Leads

  • Lead one-pager — Operational reference
  • Brief WN7 situation summary and key reminders
  • Expected Guide recovery sequence
  • Where to find BH-4419 and the Orbit serial tool, and when to use each
  • Lead responsibilities, follow-up expectations, and escalation channels
  • Lead Coaching Cues — Coaching reference
  • Strong and weak call examples*
  • Observable cues: what to listen for and what to check
  • Questions to help diagnose execution breakdowns
  • Suggested coaching responses versus issues requiring escalation

*Use approved, de-identified call excerpts where available. Otherwise, use clearly labeled illustrative scenarios grounded in documented patterns and validated by Quality/Specialist Care.

Moving Forward

  • Shared Lead message thread — a participating Lead designated by Care leadership serves as temporary thread Admin during rollout.
  • Purpose: Keep Leads, Specialist Care, and Quality aligned as WN7 situations unfold. Use the thread to ask questions, flag recurring Guide difficulties or tool blockers, share coaching observations, and communicate verified updates.
  • Actions for Leads afterwards
  • Introduce and reinforce Guide practice: In the team huddle, use the sequence slide to introduce the WN7 workflow, open dialogue with Guides, and launch the individual scenario practice. Show Guides where to access it through Sana, where to find the Call Path, and the video resource if approved. Reinforce the same decision sequence through later coaching and call reviews.
  • Use the agreed coaching cues during call reviews and Guide escalations. Surface recurring problems or blockers through the shared lead thread
  • Monitor and coach Guide execution: During existing call reviews and coaching opportunities, Leads look for observable evidence that Guides recognize the WN7 pattern, verify eligibility, choose the appropriate path, complete or arrange follow-up, and explain/document the outcome. Diagnose breakdowns, provide targeted coaching, and escalate tool or process blockers.*
    *Leads verify observed execution in the calls they review, not proficiency across every Guide.
