Executive overview

Eligible customers aren't reliably reaching recovery

The WN7 recovery path exists, but most eligible General Care calls still end in generic troubleshooting: 115 of 204 eligible calls (56%) in the September 21–30 Orbit call sample. This plan introduces a practical intervention, tests it with a small group, and expands only as evidence and capacity allow.

Reviewed WN7 calls show more consistent recognition, eligibility verification, appropriate recovery decisions, and clearly documented follow-up than the pre-rollout baseline. Customer outcomes show promising improvement, while service capacity remains manageable.
DeliveryThe intervention reaches Guides. Attendance and completion show reach, not proficiency.
Guide executionGuides recognize, verify, choose the right path, and complete or document the recovery.
Customer recoveryMore eligible customers leave with a completed recovery or owned, explained follow-up.
Operational capacityQueues, wait times, and Lead and Specialist load stay manageable.

The Guide Call Path is the central resource; everything else introduces, practices, or reinforces it.

Rollout · Phases 1–2

Prepare and Pilot

No Guide meets the intervention until required conditions are confirmed.

1

Prepare

Confirm everything Guides will depend on before any Guide meets the intervention.

Major actions

  • Validate the Guide Call Path against BH-4419 and approved guidance.
  • Put approved Plus guidance, or an authoritative escalation route, in place, validated by Account Systems and Product Ops with Specialist Care.
  • Confirm the Orbit eligibility workflow and General Care access.
  • Validate the scenario; confirm Sana delivery, native or by approved link.
  • Run the Lead working meeting for the pilot teams' Leads.
  • Confirm the Guide → Lead support route and the established fallback.
  • Select pilot teams, schedule practice time, and designate a participating Lead as temporary thread Admin.
  • Quality sets the baseline comparison approach.

2

Pilot

Put the complete intervention in front of a representative group and confirm every part reaches Guides and works.

Proposed scope: 2–3 Lead teams with a representative mix of Guides, not volunteers or only high performers. Workforce confirms the Guide count, team selection, and scheduling.

Major actions

  • Leads run the team huddle with the slide, introduce the Call Path, and launch the scenario.
  • Guides complete the scenario during scheduled work time.
  • Guides use the Call Path on live WN7 calls right away, with their Lead as first-line support.
  • Quality begins observing live WN7 calls as soon as they occur.
  • Leads raise blockers in the shared Lead thread; the Admin routes them to owners.

  • Questions, blockers, and support requests logged.

Rollout · Phases 3–5

Observe & Adjust, Expand, Sustain

Evaluate before expanding; expand wave by wave; sustain through existing coaching.

3

Observe & Adjust

Determine whether Guide execution is improving, and why or why not.

Major actions

  • Quality completes the baseline review before interpreting pilot results, then leads review of pilot calls against the six indicators (page 4).
  • Leads contribute coaching observations and diagnose before coaching: unclear next step, misread Orbit result, access or process blocker, or perceived pressure to keep troubleshooting.
  • Coach the behavior or escalate tool and access problems; refine the Call Path, scenario, or cues where evidence shows a gap.

Accountable partners

Quality (lead)Team LeadsWorkforceLearningCare Systems

Evidence / outputs

  • Indicator-based comparison with the baseline.
  • Breakdown causes and responses; refinement log.
  • Recovery and operational readout; partner recommendations.
With sufficient live-call exposure and comparable evidence, does execution support expansion?
Proceed
Meaningful improvement in Guide execution, critical blockers resolved, capacity manageable, and customer recovery outcomes directionally promising.
Adjust
The usual response to limited or mixed improvement. Refine and observe again.
Pause
Serious operational strain, inaccurate or unusable guidance, or unresolved critical access or support problems.
4

Expand

Extend in successive Lead-team waves, only as evidence and capacity allow.

Major actions

  • Expand by Lead-team wave, not as one organization-wide release.
  • Align each Lead before their Guides enter; not all 18 Leads need to attend before the pilot.
  • Repeat readiness checks per wave; carry refinements forward; keep observing with the same six indicators.

Accountable partners

Care leadershipWorkforceLearningQualityTeam Leads

Evidence / outputs

  • Readiness, execution, and capacity readout for each wave.
Every wave: does the evidence support it, and can operations absorb it?
Proceed
Care leadership finds the evidence supports expansion and Workforce approves the wave.
Adjust
A new wave shows different breakdowns. Slow down and address them.
Pause
Queue or Specialist capacity is strained.
5

Sustain

Keep execution reliable through existing coaching and maintained guidance.

Major actions

  • Leads reinforce the sequence through existing call reviews; Quality monitors recurring gaps.
  • When BH-4419, Orbit, or Plus handling change, the operational owner validates and Learning updates the resources.
  • Wind down temporary supports: the thread Admin role and Specialist thread participation.

Six observable indicators of successful execution

What Leads and Quality look for in reviewed WN7 calls, applied where relevant.

RecognitionRecognizes the potential WN7 pattern and moves toward verification
VerificationChecks eligibility and correctly interprets the result
DecisionSelects the appropriate path based on verified eligibility
RecoveryCompletes both applicable transactions, replacement and Plus, or establishes documented follow-up
CommunicationAccurately explains what was completed, what's pending, and what happens next
DocumentationRecords completed actions, outstanding work, and follow-up ownership

Four kinds of evidence, read together

DeliveryHuddle attendance; scenario access and completion.Shows reach. Not proficiency.
Guide executionThe six indicators, from reviewed calls.Shows what Guides did on the call.
Customer recoveryComplete and incomplete recovery; repeat contacts, where measurable.Shows documented recovery; repeat contacts help test whether it lasted.
Operational signalsHandle time; wait and abandonment; Lead and Specialist load.Shows capacity, not whether a customer got the right resolution.

Recovery and operational measures are proposed, pending Quality and Workforce confirmation.

What the evaluation will and won't claim

  • Same indicators, comparable call selection, applicable eligibility branches, and explicit treatment of missing evidence.
  • Observation starts with the first live calls; conclusions wait for sufficient exposure and comparable evidence.
  • Attendance or scenario completion as proof of proficiency.
  • A recorded Orbit check as proof the Guide understood the result.
  • Changes in operational metrics as proof the intervention caused them.
  • Numeric pass marks; progression rests on accountable judgment.
