One sequence for every WN7 call

Brief whole-home Wi-Fi drops on a WN7 hub or WNM-7 bundle. BH‑4419 is the authority.

  1. 1

    Recognize

    Whole home, brief drop, comes back on its own.

    Listen for the impact first. The pattern means check, not qualifies.

  2. 2

    Verify

    Hub serial into Orbit's WN7 eligibility panel.

    Read the result. Running the check isn't reading it.

  3. 3

    Decide

    Choose the path by what Orbit returned, not by the product name or symptoms.

  4. 4

    Complete

    For an eligible serial, both:
    Replacement: confirmation number
    Plus credit: shows Applied

    Either still open? Recovery isn't complete. Keep the case open.

  5. 5

    Explain & Document

    What's done, what's still open, what happens next, and who owns it.
