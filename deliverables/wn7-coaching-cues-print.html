WN7 Coaching Cues

A two-to-three-minute coaching conversation

Use it inside the call reviews and coaching you already do. One moment, one behavior, one next step.

1Notice

Name one specific moment.

“Let's look at the part right after the serial check.”

“I can see the check ran, but I can't tell what you saw next. Can you walk me through it?”

A recorded check isn't proof the Guide saw or understood the result.

2Diagnose

Find out what drove the decision.

“Walk me through what you saw after you entered the serial.”

“What did you think the next step was?”

“Was anything stopping you?”

Knowledge, interpretation, access, process, or expectations. Not every miss is a training problem.

3Practice

Try the moment again, briefly.

“Same call: the result comes back eligible. What do you do next?”

“How would you tell the customer what's done and what's still open?”

Compare the answer with the Call Path and BH-4419. Don't make up new wording.

4Commit

Agree on one thing to do next time.

“On your next WN7 call, what's the one thing you'll do differently?”

“I'll listen for that in your next review.”

If the barrier is operational, the next step is escalation to its owner, not more practice.

?Common coaching moments

Eligible, but kept troubleshooting

You noticeSerial check recorded and eligible, then generic troubleshooting to the end.

Ask“What did the result tell you?”

MoveLet the answer decide. Unsure of the next step: practice the decision on the Call Path. Felt expected to keep troubleshooting: once eligibility is verified, the restart loop stops. Couldn't open the order: escalate, don't coach.

Unsure which Orbit result counts

You noticeDuring a call review or coaching conversation, the Guide expresses uncertainty about which Orbit result determines eligibility.

Ask“Show me where you looked, and what you took it to mean.”

MoveWalk through the validated guidance together. If it doesn't answer the question, share the gap in the Lead thread. If it's an access problem, use the established route to Care Systems.

Replacement done, Plus still open

You noticeOrder confirmed; the applicable Plus action wasn't complete when the call ended.

Ask“When the call ended, what was still open, and who has it now?”

MovePractice the close: what's done, what's open, what happens next, with no timing promised.

The call can close with explained, documented, owned follow-up. The Plus transaction is still outstanding; the recovery isn't finished.

Next step unclear or not documented

You noticeThe customer sounds unsure what happens next, or notes don't show what started.

Ask“If another Guide opened this case tomorrow, what would they know?”

MovePractice a three-part recap (done, pending, next) and add it to the notes. A missed recap can mean a callback: “the second Guide had to figure out what we had promised” (Dev, Sep 18).

✓Keep it going

  • Coach one behavior at a time, against the six indicators that apply to the call.
  • Keep coaching needs separate from operational blockers.
  • Check the same behavior in your next existing call review.
  • Share recurring patterns in the Lead thread; use established escalation for blockers that need fixing now.

Huddle attendance and scenario completion show reach, not proficiency.
