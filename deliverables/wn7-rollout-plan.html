1 · Purpose and intended outcomes

Eligible customers aren't reliably reaching recovery

The WN7 recovery path exists, but most eligible General Care calls still end in generic troubleshooting. In the September 21–30 call sample, 115 of 204 eligible General Care calls (56%) took the generic troubleshooting path. This plan puts a practical intervention in place and tests it with a small group before expanding.

Reviewed WN7 calls show more consistent recognition, eligibility verification, appropriate recovery decisions, and clearly documented follow-up than the pre-rollout baseline. Customer outcomes show promising improvement, while service capacity remains manageable.

Guide execution

Guides recognize the pattern, verify eligibility, choose the right path, and complete or document the recovery.

Customer recovery

More eligible customers leave with a completed recovery, or with follow-up that is owned and explained.

Operational capacity

Queues, wait times, and Lead and Specialist load stay manageable while the change takes hold.

What's being rolled out

2 · Five-phase rollout

Phases advance on evidence, not on a calendar

Each phase ends at a decision gate: proceed, adjust, or pause. Care leadership makes each call, informed by Quality, Workforce, Learning, and Specialist Care.

1PrepareReadiness
2PilotIntroduce & practice
3Observe & AdjustEvaluate execution

5SustainReinforce & maintain
At each decision gate: Proceed · Adjust · PauseNo fixed durations; each gate opens when its evidence is in.
1

Prepare

Confirm everything Guides will depend on is in place before any Guide meets the intervention.

Major actions

  • Validate the Guide Call Path against BH-4419 and approved guidance.
  • Put approved Plus guidance, or an authoritative escalation route, in place.
  • Confirm where the eligibility result appears in Orbit and that General Care has access.
  • Develop and validate the digital scenario; confirm Sana can deliver it, natively or by approved link.
  • Run the Lead working meeting for the pilot teams' Leads.
  • Confirm the Guide → Lead live support route and the established fallback.
  • Select pilot teams and Guides; schedule practice time.
  • Designate a participating Lead as temporary thread Admin.
  • Set the baseline comparison approach (see section 3).

Decision gate

Are all required readiness conditions confirmed?

ProceedAll five are confirmed:
  • Approved Call Path and Plus guidance or escalation route
  • Validated Orbit workflow and access
  • Scenario delivery confirmed
  • Lead alignment and working support route

AdjustOnly optional items are outstanding. Launch without them and mark them pending.
PauseAny required condition is unconfirmed. Required items aren't worked around.
2

Pilot

Put the complete intervention in front of a representative group and confirm every part reaches Guides and works.

Proposed scope: 2–3 Lead teams with a representative mix of Guides, not volunteers or only high performers. Workforce confirms the Guide count, team selection, and scheduling.

Major actions

  • Leads run the team huddle with the sequence slide, introduce the Call Path, and launch the scenario.
  • Guides complete the individual scenario during scheduled work time.
  • Guides use the Call Path on live WN7 calls right away, with their Lead as first-line support.
  • Quality begins observing live WN7 calls as soon as they occur.
  • Leads raise questions and blockers in the shared Lead thread; the Admin routes them to owners.

Accountable partners

Team LeadsWorkforceLearning OperationsQualityCare leadership

Evidence / outputs

  • Huddle attendance and scenario completion. These are delivery measures, not evidence of proficiency.
  • Questions, blockers, and support requests logged.
  • Early call observations, held for interpretation in phase 3.

Decision gate

Is the intervention reaching Guides, and are its parts usable? (Whether execution is improving is judged in phase 3.)

ContinueEvery component is reaching Guides and works as designed. Observation continues.
AdjustA component isn't landing, such as scenario access or Lead availability. Fix it before judging execution.
PauseQueue or Specialist capacity becomes unmanageable, or Guides lack a working support route.
3

Observe & Adjust

Determine whether Guide execution is improving, and why or why not, before anything expands.

Major actions

  • Quality completes the baseline review against the six indicators before interpreting pilot results.
  • Quality leads review of pilot calls against the six indicators; Leads contribute coaching observations.
  • Diagnose breakdowns before coaching: unclear next step, misread Orbit result, access or process blocker, or perceived pressure to keep troubleshooting.
  • Coach the behavior, or escalate tool and access problems to their owners.
  • Refine the Call Path, scenario, or coaching cues where evidence shows a gap.

Accountable partners

Quality (lead)Team LeadsWorkforceLearningCare Systems

Evidence / outputs

  • Indicator-based comparison of pilot calls with the baseline.
  • Documented breakdown causes and the response to each.
  • Customer recovery and operational readout, read alongside the call reviews.
  • Partner recommendations to Care leadership.

Decision gate

With sufficient live-call exposure and comparable evidence, does execution support expansion?

Proceed to ExpandMeaningful improvement in Guide execution, critical blockers resolved, capacity manageable, and customer recovery outcomes directionally promising.
AdjustThe usual response to limited or mixed improvement. Refine and observe again.
PauseSerious operational strain, inaccurate or unusable guidance, or unresolved critical access or support problems.
4

Expand

Extend the intervention in successive Lead-team waves, only as evidence and capacity allow.

Major actions

  • Expand by Lead-team wave, not as one organization-wide release.
  • Align each Lead before their Guides enter the rollout. Not all 18 Leads need to attend before the pilot.
  • Repeat the readiness checks for each new wave: Lead alignment, support route, access.
  • Carry pilot refinements forward into the Call Path, scenario, and coaching cues.
  • Keep observing with the same six indicators.

Accountable partners

Care leadershipWorkforceLearningQualityTeam Leads

Evidence / outputs

  • Readiness confirmed for each wave.
  • Execution observed holding as waves are added.
  • Capacity readout for each wave.

Decision gate · every wave

Does the evidence support the next wave, and can operations absorb it?

ProceedCare leadership finds the evidence supports expansion and Workforce approves the wave.
AdjustA new wave shows different breakdowns. Slow down and address them.
PauseQueue or Specialist capacity is strained.
5

Sustain

Keep execution reliable after the rollout through existing coaching and maintained guidance.

Major actions

  • Leads reinforce the sequence through existing call reviews and coaching.
  • Quality monitors recurring execution gaps through existing reviews.
  • Learning updates the Call Path, scenario, slide, and Lead resources when BH-4419, Orbit, or Plus handling change, once the relevant owner validates the change.
  • Wind down temporary supports: the thread Admin role and Specialist thread participation.

How we'll know it's working

Quality leads the evaluation. It starts from the evidence already in hand and compares like with like.

These are established pre-rollout findings, not scores against the six indicators below. Quality sets the comparison approach before launch and, where possible, revisits the underlying calls so baseline and pilot are judged the same way. That re-review isn't a launch prerequisite; it must be complete before pilot results are interpreted.

Six observable indicators of successful execution

The behaviors Leads and Quality look for in reviewed WN7 calls, applied where relevant to each call.

RecognitionRecognizes the potential WN7 pattern and moves toward verification
VerificationChecks eligibility and correctly interprets the result
DecisionSelects the appropriate path based on verified eligibility
RecoveryCompletes both applicable transactions, replacement and Plus, or establishes documented follow-up
CommunicationAccurately explains what was completed, what's pending, and what happens next
DocumentationRecords completed actions, outstanding work, and follow-up ownership

“Once the path is live, the important questions will be: did they recognize the pattern, check eligibility, complete both transactions, and explain what happens next?”

Malik, Quality reviewer, September 15 calibration comment

Three kinds of evidence, read together

Guide execution

The six indicators, from reviewed calls.

ShowsWhat Guides actually did on the call.

Customer recovery outcomes

Complete and incomplete recovery; repeat contacts, where measurable.

ShowsWhether the recovery was documented as complete. Repeat contacts may indicate whether the resolution held.

Operational signals

Handle time, wait time and abandonment, Lead and Specialist support load.

ShowsWhether capacity is holding, not whether a customer got the right resolution.

Recovery and operational measures are proposed and need Quality and Workforce confirmation.

What the Evidence Can (and Can't) Tell Us

  • Baseline and pilot calls are compared with the same indicators, comparable call selection, the eligibility branches that apply, and explicit treatment of missing evidence.
  • Observation starts with the first live calls; conclusions wait for sufficient exposure and comparable evidence.
  • Huddle attendance or scenario completion as proof of proficiency.
  • A recorded Orbit check as proof the Guide understood the result.
  • Changes in operational metrics as proof the intervention caused them.
  • Numeric pass marks. Expansion rests on judgment by accountable partners, not thresholds.

4 · Ownership and reinforcement

Responsibilities Across the Rollout

Learning builds and maintains the learning resources. Operational owners validate what's accurate. Care leadership authorizes progression. Beacon stays the authoritative source for approved procedures.

Care leadership

Authorizes progression

  • Makes the proceed, adjust, or pause call at each gate.
  • Owns the live Guide support arrangement.
  • Ensures participating Leads can attend the working meeting.
  • Designates the temporary thread Admin.

Workforce

Operational feasibility

  • Sets pilot and wave composition, timing, and scheduled practice coverage.
  • Approves each wave's operational feasibility.
  • Validates coverage for live support; provides capacity data.

Quality

Leads evaluation

  • Sets the comparison approach and reviews baseline and pilot calls.
  • Recommends whether performance supports expansion.
  • Validates operational content in Lead resources, with Specialist Care; monitors recurring gaps.

Learning

Builds and maintains learning resources

  • Facilitates the Lead working meeting.
  • Produces and maintains the Call Path, scenario, huddle slide, Lead one-pager, and Coaching Cues.
  • Confirms instructional readiness and recommends refinements. Doesn't approve operational policy.

Team Leads

First-line support and reinforcement

  • Run the team huddle and launch practice.
  • Provide first-line live support to Guides.
  • Coach through existing call reviews; contribute observations and help diagnose gaps.

Specialist Care / policy owners

Validate procedures

  • Validate recovery procedures and Plus handling within their authority.
  • Confirm escalation availability, subject to capacity.
  • Assess whether Specialist support remains sustainable.

Learning Operations

Scenario delivery

  • Confirms assignment, access, and delivery through Sana, natively or by approved link.

Care Systems

Orbit

  • Validates Orbit access, tool behavior, and related system instructions.

Specialist capacity: two separate approvals

Mateo (Specialist Care) decides each on its own. Neither implies the other.

A · Lead working meetingSpecialists support the small-group case discussion.
B · Temporary thread supportSpecialists clarify from approved guidance during rollout, sized to capacity.

If capacity allows only one, the Lead meeting comes first: it builds the Lead capability that supports Guides through the whole rollout.

Temporary shared Lead thread

During the initial rollout, Leads need a shared place to surface questions, resolve conflicting guidance, and identify recurring blockers. A temporary coordination thread keeps those discussions visible and routes them to the appropriate owners, rather than creating another informal source of answers.

A participating Lead, designated by Care leadership, serves as Admin during rollout.

  • Coordinates questions and routes blockers to the right owners.
  • Tracks unresolved issues and recurring patterns.
  • Circulates verified answers to participating Leads.

For Lead coordination only. It doesn't replace live Guide → Lead support or established escalation, and it isn't a permanent channel.

Keeping it current

BH-4419, Orbit, or Plus handling changes→Operational owner validates→Learning updates resources→Leads reinforce in coaching→Quality watches for recurring gaps
