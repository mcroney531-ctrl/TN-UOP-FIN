WN7 Call Path

1

Recognize

Could this be the WN7 issue?

  • The whole home drops for a short time.
  • It comes back on its own.
  • It keeps happening.
  • They have a WN7 hub or a WNM-7 bundle.

Listen for the impact first. Acknowledge what the drops are disrupting before you move to the serial.Example: “I can see how frustrating it is when your connection keeps dropping, especially when you're trying to work.”

The pattern tells you to check eligibility. It doesn't mean the customer qualifies, and other causes still exist.

2

Verify

Check the serial, then read the result.

  • Ask for the hub serial, from the Northstar app or the hub itself.
  • Enter it in Orbit's WN7 eligibility panel.
  • Read the result: Eligible or Not eligible. The model code alone doesn't decide it.

Running the check isn't the same as reading it. If you can't find the serial, the tool won't load, or you're not sure which value is the result, use No clear result.

Before release: Care Systems confirms where the result appears in the panel and that General Care has access.

✓ Eligible: recovery path

3Decide

  • Follow the WN7 recovery path in BH‑4419.
  • Don't ask the customer to repeat restart steps to qualify.

4Complete both actions

Replacement
  • Submit the hub replacement order in Orbit.
  • Check it has a confirmation number.
  • Use the shipping estimate shown on the order.
Plus
  • Attach the Plus benefit as BH‑4419 describes.
  • Check the credit shows Applied.
  • Plus is a goodwill benefit, not a fix for the hub.
No confirmation number, or the credit doesn't show Applied? The recovery isn't complete.
  • Keep the recovery case open.
  • Document what went through and what didn't.
  • Arrange follow-up through the unresolved recovery queue, as BH‑4419 describes.
  • Tell the customer what's still open. Don't promise timing you can't confirm.

You can end the call with owned follow-up in place. The open item stays open.

Before release: Account Systems and Product Ops confirm how to follow up a credit that hasn't failed but isn't Applied yet.

Then go to Explain & Document.

– Not eligible: standard support

3Decide

  • Continue the right troubleshooting or escalation for the issue they reported.
  • Don't promise a replacement or the Plus benefit. The product name and symptoms don't qualify them.

4Complete

  • Finish the standard support steps for the issue.

Then go to Explain & Document.

5

Explain & Document

Before you close, cover these.

  • What's done? For example, the replacement confirmation and the shipping estimate shown on the order.
  • What's still open? Name it plainly, including Plus status.
  • What happens next? Only what's confirmed.
  • Who owns the follow-up? Say it, and note it.
  • Could the next Guide pick this up from your notes alone?

Check that the customer understands the next step.
