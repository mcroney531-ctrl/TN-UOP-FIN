WN7 Call Path

Brief whole-home Wi-Fi drops on a WN7 hub or WNM-7 bundle · BH-4419 is the authority

1 Recognize→2 Verify→3 Decide→4 Complete→5 Explain & Document

1Recognize

  • Whole home drops for a short time, comes back on its own, keeps happening.
  • WN7 hub or WNM-7 bundle.

Listen for the impact first. Acknowledge the disruption before moving to the serial.

The pattern means check, not qualifies. Other causes still exist.

2Verify

  • Ask for the hub serial, from the Northstar app or the hub.
  • Enter it in Orbit's WN7 eligibility panel.
  • Read the result: Eligible or Not eligible. The model code alone doesn't decide it.

Running the check isn't reading it. No serial, tool down, or unsure of the result? Use No clear result.

3Decide & 4Complete by what Orbit returned

✓ Eligible recovery path

  • Follow the WN7 recovery path in BH-4419. Don't ask for restart steps to qualify.

Complete both

ReplacementSubmit the hub order in Orbit; check for a confirmation number; use the shipping estimate shown on the order.
PlusAttach as BH-4419 describes; check the credit shows Applied. A goodwill benefit, not a fix.
Either didn't go through? Not complete. Keep the recovery case open, document what did and didn't go through, arrange follow-up through the unresolved recovery queue, and tell the customer what's open, with no unconfirmed timing. You can end the call with owned follow-up; the open item stays open.

– Not eligible standard support

  • Continue the right troubleshooting or escalation for the reported issue.
  • Don't promise a replacement or the Plus benefit. Product name and symptoms don't qualify anyone.
  • Finish the standard support steps.

? No clear result don't guess

  • Make no promise that depends on eligibility.
  • Recheck the serial and result against BH-4419.
  • Still unsure? Ask your Lead, or use the established fallback.
  • No serial or tool unavailable: open an eligibility review case as BH-4419 describes and give the follow-up expectation it specifies.
  • Can't open the panel or order: tell your Lead so it's escalated through existing channels.

5Explain & Document: before you close

  • What's done? e.g., the replacement confirmation and the shipping estimate on the order
  • What's still open? Name it plainly, including Plus status
  • What happens next? Only what's confirmed
  • Who owns the follow-up? Say it and note it
  • Could the next Guide pick it up from your notes alone?
  • Invite a repeat-back of anything unclear
