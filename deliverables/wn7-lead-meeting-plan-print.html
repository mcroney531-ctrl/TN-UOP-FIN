WN7 Lead Alignment: 25-Minute Working Meeting

A working discussion, not a lecture. Two cases, one documented and one composite, do the teaching; Leads leave aligned on what good execution looks like and how to coach or escalate when it breaks down.

For Learning facilitatorsProposed meeting design · owner confirmations pending

Who and when

  • Participants: the Leads whose Guides are entering the next rollout wave. Every Lead attends before their own Guides start; not all 18 need to attend before the pilot.
  • Roles: Learning facilitates. Care leadership ensures participating Leads can attend. Specialist Care supports only with Mateo's approval for meeting support, so don't assume Specialists will be there.
  • Authority: approved guidance in Beacon, including BH-4419, stays authoritative. The meeting aligns practice; it doesn't create policy.

Leads leave able to

  1. Recognize what correct WN7 recovery execution looks like on a call.
  2. Tell a knowledge gap apart from an Orbit-reading, access, or process problem.
  3. Decide what to coach and what to escalate.
  4. Introduce and reinforce the intervention with their Guides.

Before the meeting

  • Print or share the two case cards (page 2). The written cases are enough to run the discussion.
  • Have BH-4419 and the sequence slide on hand; bring the Guide Call Path if it's ready.
  • Confirm whether Specialist support is approved. If yes, form small groups with a Specialist in each; if not, run as one group.
  • Optional: a de-identified Case A recording, only if available and approved by Quality.

Validation safeguards

  • Plus status: say only that the credit had not been applied at the documented point. Don't call it pending, failed, rejected, or completed. Account Systems and Product Ops are the proposed partners for validating transaction status and approved wording, with Specialist Care's operational input.
  • Follow-up ownership: no owner is named. Leads identify what needs an owner; final operational ownership is pending validation.
  • Orbit: discuss only the documented eligibility-check facts. Don't describe screens, fields, or result locations. Care Systems confirms the live workflow before operational use.

Case A · ≈ 4 min

Strong recovery with a Plus complication

Documented call · Quality listening notes, Sep 24

What happened (read aloud)

  • The Guide verified an eligible serial and completed the replacement order.
  • She then noticed the Plus credit had not been applied at that point in the call.
  • She told the customer she would stay with the handoff, opened the approved follow-up, and explained the case stayed open until the credit was resolved. The caller said that was clear.
  • The call ran longer than Specialist examples because the credit system was delayed.
Ask Leads
  1. The replacement went through. Why isn't the call finished?
  2. What did she do about the unresolved part, and what did she not promise?
  3. What needs documenting so the next contact can see it?
  4. What needs an owner, and how would you confirm who that is?
  5. What would ending the call sooner have cost?
Listen forRecovery is complete when both transactions are done, or follow-up is opened and explained, not when the replacement is submitted. No unconfirmed outcome or timing promised. Done and pending both recorded. A longer call can beat a callback: “The customer called back about the free year, and the second Guide had to figure out what we had promised.” (Dev, Sep 18)

Ask Leads · required

What would you ask or check first, before deciding how to coach?

Optional probes, if time allows
  • What does this record tell you, and what doesn't it?
  • Why might the Guide have kept troubleshooting?
  • How would your coaching change depending on what you find?
Possible causes (hold until Leads offer theirs)
  • Didn't know the next step (Guide R., Sep 8)
  • Couldn't tell “which value on the screen counted as the result” (Sep 22)
  • Access or process: correct “Orbit permissions,” or didn't know the screen? (Sep 24)
  • Felt expected to troubleshoot: “I get asked why I did not use the basic steps” (Guide M., Sep 5)
  • Something else, e.g., the customer insisted
What a Lead checks first
  • The call, if recorded: did the Guide mention the result?
  • “Walk me through what you saw after you entered the serial.”
  • “What did you expect the next step to be?”
  • “Could you open the replacement order and credit action?” If not, check access with Care Systems.
  • “What made you keep troubleshooting?”

Ask Leads
  1. Which of these breakdowns do you expect most on your team?
  2. What will you listen for first in your next call review?
  3. What would you escalate rather than coach?
