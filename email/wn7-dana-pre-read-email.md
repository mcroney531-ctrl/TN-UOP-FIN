Hi Dana,

Thank you for sending Renee's Orbit exports and the background. You asked for a short video to help Guides handle upset callers without losing control of the conversation. When I worked through the material, much of the long-call pattern traced back to calls about the WN7 hub, where eligible customers often aren't reaching the recovery path. Before you open the attached materials, here's what I found and what I'd propose.

**What the evidence shows**

In a systematic Orbit sample of 204 eligible General Care calls from September 21–30, 115 (56%) went through generic troubleshooting instead of recovery. Even among the 159 calls with a serial check recorded, 70 still ended in generic troubleshooting.

A recorded check can't tell us what the Guide saw or understood, so this isn't a proven root cause. But awareness alone doesn't appear to explain the gap. Guides seem to struggle to recognize the pattern, interpret the Orbit result, and act on it consistently. That matters for upset callers too: a customer asked to repeat restarts they've already tried has good reason to be frustrated.

**Why I'm proposing more than a video**

A short video could explain the recovery path well. I don't think explanation alone will change what happens on live calls. At the September 18 shift feedback session, Micah described not remembering whether the offer included a subscription or a discount, then holding to ask a Lead: "the time on the call nobody sees when they say 'just use the script.'" Priya summed up the session as "a search problem, an eligibility problem, a conversation problem, and a completion problem."

Only part of that is a training problem. Guides also need consistent recovery guidance, alignment on Plus handling, a working Orbit workflow and access, a reliable route to their Lead when they're stuck, and reinforcement based on what happens on real calls. That's why the package is a small set of connected supports rather than one course, and why it names the owners who would need to confirm each dependency. It's also built to answer Marisol's point from October 1: more than five minutes is acceptable if it helps Guides and protects service levels, but there has to be a reason to believe it will work.

**Why Leads go first**

Guides go to their Leads for answers mid-call, so any gap in what Leads know shows up directly on calls. With roughly 900 hourly Guides on rotating schedules and limited Specialist capacity, pulling everyone into a session at once would cost real phone time without telling us whether it works.

So the proposal starts with Leads. A 25-minute working meeting built on two cases (one documented call, one composite drawn from the call data) gives them one shared standard for a complete recovery and for when to coach or escalate. Leads then introduce the sequence in the huddles they already run, using a single slide. Guides complete a short individual practice in scheduled work time and use the Call Path on live calls. The goal isn't more training for everyone. It's consistency, targeted support, and a pilot of two or three Lead teams (proposed; Workforce confirms) that shows what actually changes before anything expands.

**What's included**

The package is organized in three folders.

*01 — Start Here.* Three short overviews to read first:
- **Resource Overview:** each resource, who it's for, and how they fit together.
- **Rollout Plan Overview:** the five proposed phases and what decides each step forward.
- **Ownership Map Overview:** who owns what, what's confirmed versus proposed, and what's still open.

*02 — Detailed Planning.* The full **WN7 Rollout & Reinforcement Plan** (PDF): the rationale, phases and gates, evaluation approach, and the dependencies to confirm before launch.

*03 — Learning Resources.* The proposed materials. For Leads: the **Lead Meeting Plan**, **Lead Quick Reference** and **Coaching Cues**. For Guides: the **Guide Call Path** for live calls and **Make the Call**, an individual practice built around the harder Not eligible case. The **Huddle Sequence Slide** connects the two.

**Where to start**

I'd start with 01 — Start Here. The three overviews take a few minutes together and will show whether you want to go into the full plan, the learning resources, or both.

This is a proposal, not an approved rollout. Several pieces depend on their owners' confirmation before any Guide sees them, including the Plus guidance, the Orbit workflow and access, delivery of the practice, and pilot teams and practice time. The rollout is staged, so pilot evidence decides whether it expands, and it's modular: Guides would see the Call Path, the practice and the slide, while the other materials are for Leads.
